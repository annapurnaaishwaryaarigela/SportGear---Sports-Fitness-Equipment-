# SPORTIVA – Sports & Fitness Equipment Store

A modern, responsive and interactive Sports & Fitness Equipment E-Commerce website developed using HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript.

Sportiva provides a digital shopping experience for athletes, fitness enthusiasts and sports lovers to explore and purchase sports footwear, apparel, gym equipment, team sports products and accessories.

---

## Project Overview

The Sportiva Sports & Fitness Store is a frontend e-commerce web application designed to provide a clean, energetic and user-friendly shopping experience.

The website allows users to:

- Explore sports and fitness products
- Browse products by category
- Search for products
- Filter products by category, sport and price
- Sort products by price and rating
- View detailed product information
- Select product sizes
- Add products to the shopping cart
- Adjust product quantities
- Calculate cart totals
- Add products to a wishlist
- View product ratings and reviews
- Complete a simulated checkout process
- Select a simulated payment method
- Track simulated orders
- Login/Register using simulated authentication
- Contact the store through a contact form
